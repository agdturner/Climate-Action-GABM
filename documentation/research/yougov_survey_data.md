**Source:** "From Climate Crisis to Fascism? An Empirical Examination of Ecofascism in the UK" and its Supplementary Information.

| Field | Detail |

| Timing | Late April 2024 |
| Mode | Online survey experiment |

| Design | Randomised control trial (explorative experimental analyses) |
| Pre-registration | Spaiser & Dunn (2024), OSF Registries |

## 2. Experimental Design

Participants were randomly assigned to one of four groups. YouGov employed **stratified random assignment** to balance demographics across groups.

| Group | n | Stimulus |
|---|---|---|
| Control | 491 | Light-hearted video ("Toast"), unrelated to climate change |
| Climate Change Threat Treatment | 497 | First 1 min 23 sec of a video on climate change threats in the UK (village facing decommissioning due to sea-level rise) |
| Climate Justice Treatment | 498 | Video explaining climate change as an international justice issue and implications for wealthier countries like the UK |
| Active Hope Treatment | 481 | Video promoting hope for people coming together to tackle climate change |

**Procedure sequence:**

1. Informed consent & video processing ability test
2. Basic values measurement (authoritarian predisposition, dominance predisposition, biospheric values) — measured **before** intervention
3. Video intervention
4. Post-intervention measures: emotional responses (worry, anger, hope), RWA attitudes, SDO, EDO, anomie, climate policy attitudes, ecofascism items
5. Debriefing

**Treatment dummy construction:** Climate Change Threat + Climate Justice groups coded as 1; Control + Active Hope groups coded as 0 (Active Hope showed no discernible treatment effect vs. Control).

## 5. Cluster Analysis Results

A **k-means cluster analysis** on the six ecofascism items identified **4 optimal clusters**.

### 5.1 Cluster Proportions

| Cluster | % of sample | Description |
|---|---|---|
| EcoFascism Susceptible | 21.9% | High values across all six ecofascism items (relatively low on Anti-Democracy). Most clearly distinguishable cluster. |
| Inbetweens | 36.7% | Much weaker susceptibility; potentially swayable toward some ecofascist ideas. Largest cluster. |
| Climate Authoritarians | 22.9% | Very low on eco-racist items but high on Climate Niche and Strong Leader. Highly concerned about climate change; favour climate authoritarianism. Most amorphous cluster — overlaps with Inbetweens and Not Susceptible. |
| Not Susceptible | 18.5% | Reject ecofascist worldviews. Still responsive to Climate Niche argument. Smallest cluster. |

### 5.2 Cluster Quality

- Cluster silhouette plot: clusters clearly distinguishable, though some data points near decision boundaries.
- EcoFascism Susceptible cluster has fewest overlaps (only slight overlap with Inbetweens).
- Climate Authoritarians cluster is most amorphous (overlaps with Inbetweens and Not Susceptible, but not EcoFascism Susceptible).

### 5.3 Dummy Variable

For multivariate analyses: **1** = EcoFascism Susceptible cluster; **0** = all others.
